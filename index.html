Research Interests

Numerical Linear Algebra Randomized Algorithms Machine Learning Scientific Computing

My research bridges numerical linear algebra and machine learning by (i) exploiting matrix structure arising in neural network training and (ii) developing randomized numerical linear algebra methods for data compression. The goal is to design fast, stable optimizers and accelerate training from a numerical linear algebra perspective.
